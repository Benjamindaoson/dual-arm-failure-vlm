## Why

The frozen REBOOT critic study is currently framed and audited for a different, single-blind workshop. WEBP instead selects double-blind papers for what they teach about non-policy substrate choices. The existing prediction artifacts support a narrower and more relevant evaluation-interface finding, but the manuscript and submission gate must be revised without changing the experiments.

## What Changes

- Reframe the critic as a small model organism for output-contract, parser, and post-training evaluation-substrate sensitivity.
- Make the fixed-checkpoint C2 schema contrast and the Tier-B metric-dependent conclusion central, retaining Base false alarms and internal-exposure limitations.
- Add a compact reporting checklist and compress the unrun V2 RL discussion.
- Certify the WEBP double-blind CoRL submission mode and four-page main-text limit from the user-provided official template and workshop call.
- Extend the read-only audit to verify the 43-ID fixed-checkpoint contrast and distinguish PDF readiness from OpenReview form readiness.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `webp-submission`: Evidence-bound WEBP manuscript, anonymous PDF, venue-format checks, and source-bound audit.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Frozen evaluation, split, and experiment requirements remain unchanged.

## Impact

`paper/main.tex`, `paper/README.md`, submission build/audit scripts and tests, generated audit JSON, and WEBP-specific submission notes. No training code, checkpoints, splits, or metric definitions change.
